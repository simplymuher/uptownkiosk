WELCOME TO UPTOWN KIOSK GROUP

INTRODUCTION

Welcome to the official website of Uptown Kiosk Group. We are a community-based self-help group established to promote unity, cooperation, savings, entrepreneurship and community development. The group provides members with an opportunity to work together, share ideas, support one another and improve their social and economic well-being through collective action.

Since our establishment on 31st July 2026, Uptown Kiosk Group has remained committed to empowering members through responsible leadership, financial discipline, innovation and mutual respect.

OUR VISION

To build a united, disciplined and financially empowered community where every member has the opportunity to achieve sustainable social and economic growth.

OUR MISSION

To mobilize members towards savings, entrepreneurship, investment, welfare support and community service while promoting transparency, accountability and responsible leadership.

OUR CORE VALUES
  • Unity
  • Integrity
  • Transparency
  • Accountability
  • Respect
  • Teamwork
  • Innovation
  • Community Service
OUR OBJECTIVES
  • Promote unity and cooperation among members.
  • Encourage savings and investment.
  • Support entrepreneurship and business growth.
  • Promote welfare and social support.
  • Develop sustainable community projects.
  • Create opportunities for youth and women empowerment.
  • Partner with government and development organizations.
  • Encourage leadership, education and skills development.
OUR COMMITMENT

Uptown Kiosk Group is committed to building a strong, inclusive and progressive organization where every member is valued and given an opportunity to participate in decision-making and development activities. We believe that working together creates lasting solutions for individuals, families and the wider community.

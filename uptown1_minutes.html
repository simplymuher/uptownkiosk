MINUTES
1. OPENING PRAYER

The meeting commenced with an opening prayer seeking God's guidance, wisdom, unity and blessings upon all members and the activities of Uptown Kiosk Group. Members prayed for success, peace and prosperity as they officially established the group.

2. INTRODUCTION OF MEMBERS

Each member introduced themselves by stating their full name, occupation, place of residence and their interest in joining the group. Members welcomed one another and affirmed their commitment to work together in promoting the objectives of Uptown Kiosk Group.

3. FORMATION OF UPTOWN KIOSK GROUP

Members unanimously resolved to establish Uptown Kiosk Group as a community self-help organization. The group shall promote unity, savings, entrepreneurship, mutual support and community development while improving the social and economic welfare of all members.

4. ELECTION OF OFFICIALS

The meeting conducted democratic elections and elected the following officials to lead the group:

  • Chairperson
  • Vice Chairperson
  • Secretary
  • Treasurer

The elected officials accepted their responsibilities and pledged to serve the members with honesty, accountability, transparency and commitment in accordance with the Constitution.

5. ADOPTION OF THE CONSTITUTION

The proposed Constitution was read, discussed and reviewed by all members. After considering the recommendations presented during the meeting, members unanimously adopted it as the official governing document of Uptown Kiosk Group and agreed to abide by its provisions.

6. MEMBERSHIP REGISTRATION

Members agreed that every person joining the group shall complete the official membership application form, provide the required personal information and pay the agreed registration fee and membership contributions. The Secretary shall maintain an updated membership register.

7. ANY OTHER BUSINESS (A.O.B.)

Members discussed future income-generating projects, savings programmes, welfare support, community development initiatives, partnerships and training opportunities. It was agreed that additional proposals and projects would be discussed and approved during subsequent meetings.

8. CLOSING PRAYER

There being no further business, the Chairperson thanked all members for their attendance, participation and commitment. The meeting was officially adjourned with a closing prayer seeking God's continued guidance, unity and success for Uptown Kiosk Group.

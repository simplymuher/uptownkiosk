UPTOWN KIOSK GROUP CONSTITUTION ADOPTED ON 31st JULY 2026

ARTICLE 1: NAME

The name of the organization shall be Uptown Kiosk Group. The group shall operate as a voluntary, non-political and non-profit community self-help organization dedicated to improving the social and economic well-being of its members through cooperation, savings and development initiatives.

ARTICLE 2: VISION

To build a united, disciplined and financially empowered community where members support one another, promote entrepreneurship and contribute positively to sustainable social and economic development.

ARTICLE 3: MISSION

The mission of Uptown Kiosk Group is to mobilize members towards savings, investment, business development, welfare support and community service while promoting integrity, accountability, transparency and responsible leadership.

ARTICLE 4: OBJECTIVES
  • To promote unity, cooperation and mutual respect among members.
  • To encourage a savings culture and responsible financial management.
  • To establish income-generating projects that benefit members.
  • To support members during emergencies, illness, bereavement and other welfare needs.
  • To promote entrepreneurship, innovation and business opportunities.
  • To mobilize financial and material resources for community development.
  • To partner with government agencies, NGOs and private organizations for development programmes.
  • To promote education, skills development and capacity building among members.
  • To uphold honesty, integrity and accountability in all group activities.
ARTICLE 5: MEMBERSHIP

Membership shall be open to any person who supports the objectives of Uptown Kiosk Group, is of good character and is willing to comply with this Constitution.

Every applicant shall complete the prescribed membership application form, pay the approved registration fee and be admitted upon approval by the members during a duly convened meeting.

Membership may cease through voluntary resignation, death or expulsion in accordance with this Constitution.

ARTICLE 6: RIGHTS OF MEMBERS
  • Attend and participate in all meetings.
  • Vote on matters affecting the group.
  • Contest for any elective office subject to this Constitution.
  • Receive accurate financial and administrative reports.
  • Access services, programmes and opportunities offered by the group.
  • Present proposals and recommendations for the benefit of the group.
ARTICLE 7: RESPONSIBILITIES OF MEMBERS
  • Observe and respect this Constitution.
  • Attend meetings regularly unless prevented by genuine reasons.
  • Pay membership fees and agreed monthly contributions on time.
  • Promote unity, peace and cooperation among members.
  • Protect the reputation, property and resources of the group.
  • Support decisions lawfully made during meetings.
  • Avoid actions that may bring the group into disrepute.
ARTICLE 8: LEADERSHIP

The affairs of the group shall be managed by elected officials comprising:

  • Chairperson
  • Vice Chairperson
  • Secretary
  • Treasurer

Officials shall be elected democratically during the Annual General Meeting and shall serve a term of two (2) years. They may be re-elected if members are satisfied with their performance.

Officials shall perform their duties honestly, diligently and in the best interest of the group.

ARTICLE 9: MEETINGS

Ordinary meetings shall be held once every month on dates agreed upon by members.

Special meetings may be convened by the Chairperson or upon written request by at least one-third of the registered members.

One-half of the registered members shall constitute a quorum for conducting official business. Decisions shall, where possible, be made by consensus or otherwise by a simple majority vote.

ARTICLE 10: FINANCE

The funds of the group shall be obtained from membership registration fees, monthly contributions, donations, grants, fundraising activities and any lawful income-generating projects approved by members.

The Treasurer shall maintain accurate financial records and present financial reports during meetings. Group funds shall only be used for purposes approved by members.

ARTICLE 11: DISCIPLINE

Any member who violates this Constitution, fails to fulfil membership obligations or engages in misconduct shall be issued with a warning. Persistent misconduct may result in suspension or expulsion after being accorded a fair hearing and approval by members.

ARTICLE 12: AMENDMENTS

This Constitution may be amended during a duly convened meeting by a two-thirds (2/3) majority vote of members present, provided that notice of the proposed amendment has been communicated to members before the meeting.

ARTICLE 13: DISSOLUTION

The group may only be dissolved by a three-quarters (3/4) majority vote of all registered members. Upon dissolution, all liabilities shall first be settled before any remaining assets are distributed or transferred in accordance with the resolution of members and applicable laws.

ARTICLE 14: ADOPTION

This Constitution was read, discussed and unanimously adopted by the founding members of Uptown Kiosk Group during the inaugural meeting held on 31st July 2026. It shall take effect immediately upon adoption and shall remain the supreme governing document of the group until lawfully amended.
